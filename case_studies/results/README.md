# Archived study results

These are the frozen outputs for the three residential case studies used as the study basis. They were generated on 30 August 2026 and copied into stable GitHub-friendly filenames without changing workbook contents or numerical data.

Folders:

- `Stonecrete/`
- `Bamboo/`
- `Bamboo_Attic/`

The additional independent Balpremi School analysis is archived as `BOM_Balpremi_School_Independent.zip`, containing its results workbook, contribution figures, and Monte Carlo draws. It is separate from the three frozen residential case studies.

The raw Monte Carlo files are compressed CSVs because they contain the run-level simulation draws used for uncertainty analysis.
